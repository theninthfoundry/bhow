# 16 — Creative direction / Design that earns attention, not distracts from care

## The idea in one sentence

**Bhow feels like an exquisite pocket field journal whose pages grow with the dog.** Little ink-drawn details echo a field notebook; editorial typography and controlled asymmetry communicate confidence. The dog's own real photograph is the emotional focal point; if no photo exists, custom vector marks and type carry the design.

## Signature visual system

- Use two modes: **Field** (warm textured paper, stamped micro-labels, hairline rules) and **Clinical** (clear white/ink, unambiguous hierarchy) based on task. Emergency and safety surfaces always Clinical.
- Small haldi stripe denotes one most important next action. No gradient buttons, giant floating paws, emoji overload, marketplace-style product grids or fake hearts.
- Original **scent trail** interaction: tiny line segments connect *real recorded* moments as the user scrolls a timeline; never mimic an ECG or diagnostic waveform.
- Original **portrait lens**: dog's consented photo on onboarding, subtly zooming no more than 3% over 250ms; respects reduced motion. Framed with descriptive location and date only when intentionally recorded and not public.
- **Passport leaf turn**: vertical chronological transitions; each record has provenance label and distinct ruled margin. Prioritize mobile stability over visual spectacle.
- **Breed constellation** (later): labeled, original procedural vector nodes describing visible attributes (ears, coat, silhouette), with uncertainty as text. Not a scientifically validated explanation of model attribution without dedicated interpretability work.
- **Yearbook** (later): optional export made from user's own photos and care milestones, not public-by-default social sharing.

## Interactive art direction guidelines

Motion choreography 160–280ms, reduced-motion support. Confident motion appears in delightful low-risk moments only; health warnings appear immediately. Use original abstract vector paths and lightweight SVG shapes (drawn by the team), not AI-generated photography/illustrations. Typography should communicate human warmth more than decoration.

## Component variants worth crafting carefully

`DogHero`: scalable from name/initials to real-photo ratio. `NoteRail`: chronology with one vertical rule and occasion markers. `CredibilityChip`: caregiver / vet / unknown distinct from urgency chip. `CareTicket`: a tear-off typographic treatment for exported appointment summary. `FoodVerdict`: oversized word `FLAGGED` or `UNKNOWN`, clear rationale and contact action. `NeighborhoodIndex`: verified places with stale-data labels. `GentleEmptyState`: useful prompts, not mascot shame.

## Copywriting voice

Short, precise, caring, never infantilizing: `You added a visit.` not `Pawsome!`. `We don't know if this is safe.` not `100% safe`. `Your vet can help confirm.` not `AI knows your dog's needs`. When unknown: explicit. When the next action matters: one bold verb.

## Art production and copyright policy

No AI-generated imagery; no unlicensed stock photography; no copied competitor hero graphics. Source/commission original vector symbols and accessible labels. Verify licenses for every font and source photo. User dog photos private by default; user may grant display for their own private profile without licensing them to Bhow for marketing/training.
