# 17 — Discovery and validation interviews

## Research questions

Does care-record fragmentation cause recurring cost or stress? Which single job is most painful? Does first-time onboarding feel welcoming or data-hungry? Are people comfortable sharing a vet-ready passport? When they face uncertain dog food, who do they trust? What commercial service, if any, would they pay for? Don't ask only whether they “love the idea.”

## Recruitment plan

Target approximately 10 first-time owners, 10 experienced caregivers, 5 rescue/community feeders and 3–5 veterinary professionals. Recruit varied income, phone capability, breeds including indies, age of dog and dog-care roles. These are qualitative research targets, not a representative quantitative sample.

## 20–30 minute owner interview script

1. Tell me about the last time your dog needed veterinary care. Walk me through each step.
2. Where do vaccine slips, prescriptions and appointments live today? Can you show the workflow (without sharing private content with us)?
3. What's an important dog-care detail you needed but couldn't find?
4. How do you decide whether your dog's food is appropriate? When did this last become confusing?
5. What do you do if you think an unfamiliar food could be harmful?
6. Who else sometimes cares for this dog? How do instructions get handed off?
7. What tools have you tried? What made you stop using them?
8. What has cost you time or money in the last 30 days?
9. Show two prototype flows: add care event and find vet-ready summary. Where do you hesitate?
10. Would you voluntarily use this again next week? For what precise action? What would make you pay, if anything?

## Vet interview (separate)

Ask how history is received, which evidence is trusted, what client-collected data creates work, whether QR record handoff would help, what must NEVER be implied by an app, constraints on verification, and how a referral directory should be audited. Capture clinic operations and credential verification requirements separately.

## Prototype experiment card

**Hypothesis:** an appointment-led first action activates caregivers better than photo-first breed discovery. **Test:** moderated side-by-side first-use prototypes with randomized order (nonclinical only). **Measure:** time to meaningful record, task completion without hints, trust language misunderstandings, follow-up intention plus actual week-later use if consented. **Decision:** choose the path with higher completion and fewer trust errors; do not overinterpret small sample statistics.

## Evidence tracker template

`Interview ID | Role | Current workflow | Frequency | Pain severity | Existing workaround | Quote (permissioned) | Feature asked for | Work observed | Next experiment`. Store consent and identifying recordings outside public repo. Do not use high-level TAM statistics as a substitute for observed demand.
